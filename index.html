What I do

I build React and TypeScript product applications, and design systems in code: design tokens, component APIs, and the rules that keep a system from drifting once several people are shipping into it.

I direct AI coding agents against a method and a review I wrote myself, and I read every diff before it ships. AI moved the bottleneck from typing to judgement. Judgement is the part I am good at, and the responsibility stays with me.

Selected work

cuewigs — I wrote the frontend. The GSAP implementation, the responsive system and the performance are mine; the design and the motion spec came from the designer. The layout scales as one continuous system: everything in rem, the root font-size interpolated against the viewport, so nothing jumps between breakpoints. That one was my call. Awwwards Developer Award and Site of the Day, February 2024 — Awwwards gives that one to the developer.

kasiawalter.it — this site. A real-time engine sharing a render loop with a React app: Three.js and my own GLSL, written from scratch in TypeScript. It holds on a mid-range phone because I made it hold — the GPU is tiered and effects are dropped by cost, scroll work is rAF-coalesced, and layout is read once a frame. Reduced motion switches the engine off and the page still reads. Built by directing AI agents with persistent memory, every diff reviewed by me.

Design-direction methodology — an agent skill of my own, written in my own time and used in production work: research in four lenses, a semantic brief with an approval gate, reverse-reading as the selection step, and blind subagents generating competing directions. Nobody assigned it. I wrote it because directing agents without a method produces confident, generic work.
